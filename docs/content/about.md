## 公司介绍 | Company Introduction

```
H2:     Foshan Bodian Glass Co., LTD
正文：
Foshan Bodian Glass Co., Ltd. was established in 2018, located in
Shishan Town, Nanhai District, Foshan City, Guangdong Province. The
company focuses on the production and processing of insulating glass,
tempered glass, laminated glass and craft glass. Products are widely used
in home decoration, construction and other fields.

All glass products have passed the Chinese Safety Glass Certification
Center inspection and obtained "National Compulsory Product Certification"
(3C) certification. Bodian glass not only serves the domestic market, but
also actively expands the international market, providing one-stop glass
solutions for global customers.

## 原材料优势 | High Quality Raw Materials

```
H2:     High Quality Raw Materials

### 玻璃原片 | Glass Original Sheets
We use Xinyi, South Glass and other big brand original film. Usually with
higher strength, good flatness and uniformity, and low iron content, which
can reduce the risk of self-explosion.

### 丁基胶 | Butyl Rubber
As the first dense process in the manufacturing of insulating glass, the
sealing material mainly plays the role of water vapor sealing. High quality
butyl glue has very low water vapor transmittance.

### 硅酮胶 | Silicone Sealant
We use first-line brand silicone sealant. High quality silicone gel has
good weather resistance, vibration resistance, and adhesion to a variety
of materials, making it an ideal choice for hollow glass external sealing.
```

## 业务覆盖 | Business Coverage

```
H2:     Business Coverage

### 国内到国际 | Domestic to International
The company has a solid foundation in the domestic market, providing
quality glass products and services to customers nationwide. With the
advancement of globalization, we actively expand overseas markets.
Products are exported to Australia, Africa, the Middle East, Cambodia,
Vietnam and other countries.

### 线下到线上 | Offline to Online
With a perfect offline sales and service network, we provide customers
with direct product experience. With the development of e-commerce, the
company also actively builds online platforms, expands sales channels
through the Internet, and provides convenient online consultation and
customer service.

### 家装到工程 | Home Decoration to Engineering
We focus on the field of home decoration, providing customized door and
window glass products. For engineering projects, the company has rich

decorative glass, etc.
