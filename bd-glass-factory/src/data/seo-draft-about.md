## 5. 公司介绍长文案（Company Introduction，约 800 字）

### 5.1 开篇段落

Foshan Bodian Glass Co., Ltd. (BDGLASS) is a professional glass manufacturer based in Foshan City, Guangdong Province, China — the heart of the world's glass manufacturing industry. Established in 2018, we have grown from a small glass processing workshop into a modern glass factory with two production bases totaling 15,000 square meters, serving clients across China and exporting to over 10 countries worldwide.

### 5.2 核心产品

Our primary product lines include **tempered glass** (toughened safety glass, 3-19mm), **insulating glass** (IGU / double glazed units, 12-52mm), **laminated glass** (PVB/SGP safety glass, 6.38-60mm), and **craft glass** (decorative glass with acid etching, silk screen printing, digital enamel, hot melting, and stained glass techniques). These products serve three main market segments: residential home decoration, commercial building construction, and architectural curtain wall projects.

### 5.3 生产实力

Our factory is equipped with industry-leading glass processing machinery including a **SouthTech tempering furnace** (capable of processing glass up to 3,300 × 8,000mm), **5 automated hollow glass production lines** for IGU manufacturing, **CNC bending machines** for precision curved glass, **high-precision CNC cutting machines**, and **automatic edge grinding machines**. This comprehensive equipment portfolio allows us to handle everything from standard flat glass processing to complex custom architectural glass fabrication under one roof.

### 5.4 原材料与质量控制

Quality begins with raw materials. We source our glass original sheets from China's top-tier float glass manufacturers — **Xinyi Glass** and **South Glass (CSG)** — ensuring higher strength, excellent flatness and uniformity, and low iron content that reduces the risk of spontaneous breakage in tempered glass. For insulating glass production, we use first-line brand **butyl rubber** as the primary seal (providing extremely low water vapor transmittance) and high-quality **silicone sealant** for the secondary seal, ensuring 10+ years of seal integrity.

Every batch of glass undergoes strict quality inspection before leaving our factory. Our QC process includes dimensional accuracy verification, surface defect inspection, optical quality testing, impact resistance testing (for tempered glass), and seal integrity verification (for insulating glass). All products are certified by the **Chinese Safety Glass Certification Center** and carry the **National Compulsory Product Certification (3C)** mark.

### 5.5 出口市场

While we maintain a strong presence in China's domestic market, BDGLASS has been actively expanding internationally since our founding. Our glass products are exported to **Australia, Africa, the Middle East, Cambodia, Vietnam, and other Southeast Asian countries**. We provide professional export packaging (plywood crates with cork separators), full documentation support, and flexible shipping terms including FOB, CIF, and DDP.

### 5.6 团队文化

Our team of 80+ employees has an average age of 29, bringing together youthful energy with technical expertise. We invest continuously in employee training and skill development, ensuring our production team stays current with the latest glass processing technologies and quality standards. The company culture emphasizes that employees are not just producers — they are members of the BDGLASS family.

## 11. FAQ（6 个问答）

### Q1: Where is BDGLASS factory located?

Our factory is located at No.23, North Garden Road, Shishan Town, Nanhai District, Foshan City, Guangdong Province, China. Foshan is widely recognized as one of China's largest glass manufacturing clusters, with a complete supply chain ecosystem from raw float glass to finished architectural glass products.

### Q2: What certifications does BDGLASS have?

All our safety glass products (tempered glass, insulating glass, laminated glass) are certified by the **Chinese Safety Glass Certification Center** and carry the **National Compulsory Product Certification (3C / CCC)** mark. We also comply with **EN 12150** (tempered glass), **EN 1279** (insulating glass), and **EN 14449** (laminated glass) European standards. Test reports and certificates are available for project orders.

### Q3: What is your production capacity and lead time?

Our two production bases total 15,000 square meters with 5 production lines. Standard lead times are: **tempered glass 7-15 working days**, **insulating glass 10-20 working days**, **laminated glass 10-20 working days**, and **craft glass 7-15 working days** (samples: 7-10 days). Large project orders may require 25-30 working days. Rush orders are negotiable.

### Q4: Do you accept OEM and custom orders?

Yes. Custom orders are our core business. We accept custom dimensions, thicknesses, colors, edge finishes, hole patterns, printed designs, and multi-layer combinations (tempered + laminated + insulating). Send us your specifications or design files (AI, PDF, DWG, DXF) and we will provide a detailed quotation within 24 hours.

### Q5: Which countries do you export to?

We currently export to **Australia, Africa, the Middle East, Cambodia, Vietnam, and other Southeast Asian countries**. We provide professional export packaging (plywood crates with cork separators), full customs documentation, and flexible shipping terms including FOB Tianjin, CIF, and DDP. New markets are continuously being developed.

### Q6: How can I visit your factory?

We welcome factory visits by appointment. Our factory is approximately 45 minutes by car from Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport (CAN). Please contact us via WhatsApp at +86 13723810568 or email info@bdglassfactory.com to schedule your visit. Airport pickup and hotel arrangements can be coordinated for serious buyers and project partners.
