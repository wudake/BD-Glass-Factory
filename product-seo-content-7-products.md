#### H2: What Tempered Glass Means for Your Project

Tempered glass — also called toughened safety glass — is not just stronger. It changes how you design with glass. When ordinary glass breaks, it shards into dangerous fragments. Tempered glass shatters into small, blunt granules. That difference protects people, reduces liability, and lets you use larger glass panels in high-traffic spaces without worry.

At BDGLASS, we temper glass up to **3,300 × 8,000mm** on a SouthTech tempering furnace. Every sheet undergoes uniform heating to 680°C followed by rapid air-quench cooling. The result: surface compression stress ≥ 90 MPa, impact resistance 4–5× higher than annealed glass, and thermal shock tolerance up to 200°C.
