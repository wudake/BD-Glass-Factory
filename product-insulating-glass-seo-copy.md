```
BDGLASS manufactures high-performance insulating glass units (IGU), also known as 
double glazed glass or hollow glass, at our 15,000㎡ factory in Foshan, Guangdong, 
China. Our insulating glass combines two or more glass panes separated by aluminum 
or warm-edge spacers, with the cavity filled with dry air or inert gas (argon or 
krypton) to achieve superior thermal and acoustic insulation.

## What Is Insulating Glass (IGU)?

Insulating glass is a prefabricated unit consisting of two or more glass panes 
separated by a spacer and sealed around the perimeter. The space between the panes 
is filled with dry air or inert gas to reduce heat transfer. When combined with 
Low-E (low-emissivity) coatings, insulating glass can reduce thermal transmittance 
(U-value) to as low as 1.0 W/m²K — significantly improving building energy efficiency.

## Our Insulating Glass Specifications

We supply insulating glass in total thicknesses from 12mm to 52mm, with a maximum 
processing size of 2,500mm × 5,000mm. Spacer options include 6A, 9A, 12A, 16A, and 
20A aluminum spacers or warm-edge spacers. Gas fill options include dry air, argon 
(standard), and krypton (premium). All products comply with Chinese 3C (CCC) 
certification, European EN 1279 standards, and IGCC (Insulating Glass Certification 
Council) requirements.

## Why Choose BDGLASS as Your Insulating Glass Supplier?

- **Complete In-House Production**: We produce both the glass panes (tempered, 
  laminated, or Low-E) and the insulating glass units in our own factory — ensuring 
  consistent quality and shorter lead times.
- **Low-E Coating Options**: We offer single-silver, double-silver, and triple-silver 
  Low-E coatings for different climate zones and energy code requirements.
- **10-Year Seal Warranty**: Our insulating glass units are manufactured with 
  high-quality butyl primary seal and silicone/polysulfide secondary seal, backed by 
  a 10-year warranty against seal failure.
- **Custom Configurations**: From standard double glazing to triple glazing with 
  warm-edge spacers and internal blinds — we tailor the IGU to your project needs.
- **Export Packaging**: Professional plywood crates with desiccant bags to maintain 
  seal integrity during ocean shipping.

## Common Applications

Our insulating glass is widely used in commercial building curtain walls, aluminum 
window and door systems, skylights, cold storage facilities, soundproof walls, and 
high-end residential projects. For tropical climates, we recommend Low-E coating on 
surface #2 to reduce solar heat gain. For cold climates, Low-E on surface #3 maximizes 
heat retention.
```

```
## Frequently Asked Questions About Insulating Glass

### What is the difference between insulating glass and ordinary double glazing?
Insulating glass (IGU) is a factory-sealed unit with dry air or inert gas between 
the panes, separated by a spacer with desiccant. Ordinary double glazing often refers 
to two separate panes installed in a window frame without a sealed cavity. IGU provides 
superior thermal and acoustic performance because the sealed gas layer and desiccant 
prevent condensation and maintain insulation over time.

### What is Low-E coating and do I need it?
Low-E (low-emissivity) coating is a thin metallic layer applied to the glass surface 
that reflects infrared heat while allowing visible light to pass through. For buildings 
in hot climates, Low-E reduces solar heat gain and lowers cooling costs. For cold 
climates, it reflects indoor heat back into the room. We strongly recommend Low-E 
coating for all energy-efficient building projects.

### What spacer should I choose — aluminum or warm edge?
Aluminum spacers are cost-effective and suitable for most standard applications. 
Warm-edge spacers (made of stainless steel or composite materials) have lower thermal 
conductivity and reduce heat loss at the glass edge by 30-50%. Warm-edge is recommended 
for passive house projects, green buildings, and cold climate applications.

### Can insulating glass be tempered or laminated?
Yes. The individual glass panes in an IGU can be tempered, laminated, or heat-strengthened 
depending on safety and performance requirements. For curtain walls and high-rise 
buildings, we typically use tempered outer pane + tempered inner pane. For soundproofing, 
a laminated outer pane improves acoustic performance.

### What is your MOQ and lead time for insulating glass?
Our standard MOQ is 50 square meters. Lead time is 10-20 working days depending on 
configuration complexity (Low-E coating, triple glazing, warm-edge spacer, internal 
blinds). Large project orders may require 25-30 working days.

### How do you ensure seal quality during shipping?
We use plywood crates with cork or rubber separators between each IGU. Desiccant bags 
are placed inside the crate to absorb moisture. Units are sealed with plastic film to 
prevent dust and water ingress. For ocean shipping, we recommend FOB or CIF terms with 
professional loading supervision.

### What certifications does your insulating glass have?
Our insulating glass is certified under Chinese 3C (CCC) compulsory certification. 
We also comply with EN 1279 (European insulating glass standard) and IGCC 
(Insulating Glass Certification Council) requirements. Test reports for gas retention, 
dew point, and UV transmittance are available for project orders.
```
